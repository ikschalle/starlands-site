    An amnesiac alien, all he knows is that he's from another world, but he's not sure what world, or why he's here. But whilst he's living on this planet, he's decided he might as well explore it! He's learnt a lot in the time he's spent journeying through the wild, so by the time he's arrived at the Stardoods Consortium, he's on a mission to find something that even the native planet-dwellers don't know about! What a whimsical lil guy!

    Well, as long as it doesn't involve danger. What a coward! Even if he can somewhat fight, he really doesn't want to. After all, why risk your own life when you could get someone else to do it for you? Unfortunately, his ability to actually convince people to do these things is... questionable, at best. He rapidly switches between being whiny and selfish one moment, then full of false bravado the next. But, sadly for him, he's not without morals and may return a favour if he feels inclined to. Maybe. But he's not a "softie", alright?!
