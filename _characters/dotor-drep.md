## About

A strange and eccentric being that resided in Starlands for a very long time. Despite their immortality, they find themselves struggling to remember how they were made or where they came from, just that they exist because of someone he refers to as "Them". Dotor is known to be an observer and is usually seen just standing in a corner and "staring" with their blank paper ":>" face. Some find this unnerving and no one will ever know that Dotor is just going through his "mindscape" to find resources and materials. Because of this, Dotor usually depends on themselves instead of choosing to ask others for help. Expectedly, this is an issue where Dotor often gets into dangerous situations and comes out nearly dead.
