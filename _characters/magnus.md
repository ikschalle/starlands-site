## About
> "There are many philosophies to life; there is truth in all of them."

Magnus, initially a lone traveller seeking a new land, has found a place to call home. Home is where their friends are. He is often drawn to the mysteries of the world; the Warden, the Creaking, Edward - the list is endless. He never turns down the opportunity for manual labour, yet he does not believe in overworking. 

> "Rest and work are necessary in equal amounts. On occasion, the significance of rest weighs heavier."

There are rumours that he is wanted in his homeland, but they are senseless murmurs. Magnus often travels far and wide (on foot and boat), returning with blessings from all over the world.
