## About
> "The fact that I'm still here means there's still a chance, slim it may be."

A traveler from distant lands who recently "moved into" Ninth Silence. Ever curious and goal-oriented, Penélope is always searching for answers. Above all, she wants to break the curse placed on her that keeps taunting her, tiring her, giving her nightmares, extending her lifespan, and promising that she will not stay the way she is forever. She's awkward and distrustful to most, fearing getting too attached to just about anyone. She's rarely ever relaxed, but if one were to worm one's way through her prickly outside, one may discover the rather sweet and playful woman she used to be before her life was turned inside out.
